Workflow

Five steps. Practitioner in control.

The entire pipeline runs through a secure backend. No AI vendor keys are ever stored on the device.

  1. 01

    Capture or import

    Use the device camera or import from your photo library. Metadata is stripped before upload.

  2. 02

    OCR & extraction

    The report image is processed server-side. Findings are structured and PII is filtered before any AI call.

  3. 03

    Review findings

    Inspect, edit, reorder, or remove extracted findings. You stay in full control of what proceeds to generation.

  4. 04

    Generate illustration

    A neutral, anatomical 2D illustration is generated using the reviewed, de-identified findings.

  5. 05

    Approve & save

    You review the output. Only your approved visualization is saved — encrypted on-device.

Privacy & Security

Designed for sensitive data

Every design decision in Resolara starts with data protection. Patient information never reaches an AI provider in identifiable form.

HIPAA-aligned handling

Designed with HIPAA-aligned data handling principles. No compliance claim is made without the full legal, policy, and contractual framework in place.

De-identified before AI

PII is filtered server-side before any AI processing. Raw report images and OCR text are never sent to AI providers in identifiable form.

No keys on the device

AI vendor credentials never reside in the mobile app. All processing is orchestrated through Resolara's backend proxy.

Encrypted on-device storage

Approved visualizations and metadata are encrypted at rest. Raw uploads are purged once processing is complete.

Practitioner approval required

Every visualization must be explicitly approved by the practitioner before it is saved. Clinical judgment is never automated away.

Conservative retention defaults

Retention policies default to minimal. Raw content is cleaned up automatically. Retention periods are configurable for each deployment.
