The communication gap

They got the report. They still don't know what's wrong.

Every day, patients leave clinics holding a report written for specialists — dense with clinical terminology that means nothing to them. "Posterior disc protrusion at C5/C6 with mild effacement of the anterior thecal sac." It's their own body. It reads like a foreign language.

They go home and search online. An hour later they're more frightened than informed. The gap between what you know and what they understand isn't a failure of your communication — it's a limitation of words alone.

When you use Resolara in consultation, the patient doesn't have to imagine. They see it — on screen, in context, reviewed and approved by you.

Features

Everything the practitioner needs. Nothing they don't.

Built around a single clinical workflow. Lean, focused, and private by design.

Capture or import

Photograph a report or import from your library. Image metadata is stripped before anything leaves the device.

AI-powered extraction

Clinically relevant findings are extracted and structured server-side. Patient identifiers are filtered before any AI processing.

Full practitioner review

Inspect every extracted finding before anything proceeds. Edit, reorder, or remove. You control what the illustration represents.

Anatomical illustration

A neutral, 2D anatomical illustration generated from your confirmed findings — clear enough for patients, precise enough for clinical use.

Approve before saving

Nothing is stored without your explicit sign-off. Regenerate instantly if needed. Every saved output reflects a deliberate clinical decision.

Encrypted local history

Approved visualizations are stored on-device, encrypted at rest. Raw report images are purged once processing completes.

Precision you can trust

Every finding scored. Every output reviewed.

Extraction is meticulous. Every finding comes back with a confidence score the practitioner can see, interrogate, and act on. Nothing proceeds to generation without deliberate, clinician-gated confirmation.

Confidence scored

Every extracted finding is assigned a confidence score. The practitioner sees exactly how certain the extraction is before deciding what proceeds to generation.

Practitioner-gated

No illustration is generated without explicit practitioner confirmation. The clinician reviews, edits, or removes any finding — full control, every time.

De-identified before generation

Patient identifiers are stripped server-side before the AI processes anything. The illustration is built from structured clinical findings, not raw report text.

Regeneration on demand

Not satisfied with the output? Regenerate instantly. The practitioner approves the final result — only what meets their standard is saved.

Structured extraction

Findings are extracted into a structured format with body region tagging. This precision informs a more anatomically accurate and clinically grounded illustration.

Reviewed. Approved. Saved.

The three-step final gate. Review the illustration. Approve it. Only then is it stored — encrypted, on-device, under your control.

Privacy & security

Designed for sensitive clinical data

Every design decision starts with data protection. Report content is sensitive. We treat it that way.

HIPAA-aligned handling

Designed with HIPAA-aligned data handling principles. No compliance claim is made without the full legal and contractual framework.

De-identified before AI

Patient identifiers are filtered server-side before any AI provider receives data. Raw content never reaches an AI in identifiable form.

No keys on the device

AI vendor credentials never reside in the mobile app. All processing is orchestrated through Resolara's secure backend proxy.

Encrypted on-device storage

Approved visualizations are encrypted at rest. Raw uploads are purged once processing completes. Approved outputs are the only retained artefact.

Practitioner approval required

Every visualization must be explicitly approved before it is saved. Clinical judgment is never automated away.

Conservative retention defaults

Retention defaults to minimal. Raw content is cleaned up automatically. Policies are configurable per deployment — conservative unless explicitly changed.
