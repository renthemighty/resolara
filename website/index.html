The real problem

You got the report. You still don't know what's happening.

Every day, patients arrive at consultations holding a report they cannot read. The language is dense, clinical, and written for specialists — not for the person it's about.

"Posterior disc protrusion at C5/C6 with mild effacement of the thecal sac." Your patient has been handed a description of their own body, and it might as well be a foreign language.

So they do what everyone does: they go home and search online. An hour later they're more frightened than informed, surrounded by worst-case scenarios that may have nothing to do with their situation.

This is the gap Resolara helps you close. When you use Resolara in consultation, your patient doesn't have to imagine what's happening inside their body — they see it. On screen. Clearly. In context. Reviewed and explained by you, the clinician sitting in front of them.

Precision you can trust

Every finding scored. Every output reviewed.

Resolara takes a meticulous approach to extraction. Every finding is assigned a confidence score before the practitioner ever sees it — so nothing proceeds without deliberate, clinically grounded review. The practitioner sees precisely how certain the system is, retains full editorial control over what advances to generation, and gives explicit confirmation before any illustration is produced. The output is not automated; it is practitioner-approved, every time.

Confidence scored

Every extracted finding is assigned a confidence score. The practitioner sees precisely how certain the extraction is before deciding what proceeds to generation.

Practitioner-gated

No illustration is generated without explicit practitioner confirmation. The clinician reviews, edits, or removes any finding — full control, every time.

De-identified before generation

Patient identifiers are stripped server-side before the AI ever sees the data. The illustration is built from clinically structured findings, not raw report text.

Regeneration on demand

Not satisfied with the output? Regenerate instantly. The practitioner approves the final result — only what meets their standard is saved.

Structured extraction

Findings are extracted into a structured format with body region tagging. This precision informs a more anatomically accurate illustration.

Reviewed. Approved. Saved.

The three-step final gate. Review the illustration. Approve it. Only then is it stored — encrypted, on-device, under your control.

The solution

You've spent years mastering the language of medicine. Your patients haven't. The gap between what you know and what they understand isn't a failure of communication — it's a limitation of words alone.

When a patient hears "posterior disc herniation at L4–L5 with moderate foraminal stenosis," they hear uncertainty. When they see exactly where their disc is and what it means for the nerve running beside it, they understand.

Resolara gives you an anatomical illustration — created in seconds from the report you're already holding, reviewed and approved by you before it's shown. The image carries what words alone cannot. Your consultation becomes clearer, more efficient, and more trusted.

Resolara is an educational communication tool. Outputs are anatomical illustrations for practitioner use only — not diagnostic software. All clinical judgment remains with the healthcare professional.

Use cases

Where Resolara changes the conversation

From post-imaging consultations to referral letters, there are moments in every clinical day where a visual says more than a paragraph ever could.

Post-imaging consultation

Show the patient exactly what was found

Stop describing anatomy with words and start showing it. Walk a patient through their scan findings with an illustration generated directly from the report — reviewed by you, accurate to the findings, and immediately understandable.

Referral & handover

Give the next practitioner instant context

Attach a clear anatomical illustration to a referral letter or handover summary. The receiving clinician sees the relevant anatomy at a glance — reducing the need for follow-up queries and accelerating continuity of care.

Treatment planning

Build shared understanding before you begin

When a patient can see what you're treating and why, consent becomes genuine. Use Resolara to walk through the anatomical context of a proposed intervention — so the patient understands not just what will happen, but where and why.

Clinical education

Teach anatomy in context of real cases

For clinical educators and training programmes, Resolara bridges the gap between textbook anatomy and patient presentations. Generate case-specific illustrations that make abstract structures concrete for learners at every level.

Rehabilitation & physiotherapy

Help patients understand their own recovery

Patients who understand their injury recover with more purpose. Show the structure involved, the extent of the finding, and the anatomy of the region being rehabilitated — and watch compliance improve alongside comprehension.

Second opinion & specialist review

Communicate findings across disciplines

When seeking or providing a specialist opinion, a clear anatomical illustration creates immediate alignment between clinicians — removing ambiguity from written descriptions and making the discussion more precise from the first exchange.

Privacy & security

Designed for sensitive clinical data

Every design decision in Resolara starts with data protection. Report content is sensitive. We treat it that way.

HIPAA-aligned handling

Designed with HIPAA-aligned data handling principles throughout. No compliance claim is made without the full legal, policy, and contractual framework.

De-identified before AI

Patient identifiers are filtered server-side before any AI provider receives data. Raw content never reaches an AI in identifiable form.

No keys on the device

AI vendor credentials never reside in the mobile app. All processing is orchestrated through Resolara's secure backend proxy.

Encrypted on-device storage

Approved visualizations are encrypted at rest. Raw uploads are purged once processing completes. Approved outputs are the only retained artefact.

Practitioner approval required

Every visualization must be explicitly approved before it is saved. Clinical judgment is never automated away — you remain the accountable decision-maker.

Conservative retention defaults

Retention defaults to minimal. Raw content is cleaned up automatically. Policies are configurable per deployment — but conservative unless explicitly changed.
